# Decision-oriented research upgrade

## Outcome

Each new investment review begins with an executive opinion, dated price, horizon, principal reasons, valuation status and observable revision triggers. Buy, Hold and Sell require supported reasoning; missing decision-critical evidence is **Not rated**, never an automatic Hold.

## Small implementation

1. Retain the operating model and immutable editions. Add optional presentation v4 with a Decision sheet: market capitalization, cash/debt and selected-case equity bridge; separately disclosed investment carrying amounts; residual; and two comparable annual cash-flow observations. No new valuation engine or automated rating.
2. Source the investment inventory from reviewed historical facts. Never subtract it twice when the operating model already includes other assets. Carrying amounts are not current fair values. Historical cash yields use market equity, not enterprise value, because the cash series includes corporate interest and taxes.
3. Require an explicit decision record for v4 reader editions. Bind the visible opinion to the manifest and prevent an illustrative model from publishing a definitive price-based Buy/Hold/Sell. Preserve validation of older editions.
4. Investigate MLX's latest asset, project and peer disclosures; explain value drivers, competing explanations, coherent downside, funding uncertainty and the next evidence needed. Retain bounded X checks and exact quoted excerpts with qualifications.
5. Regenerate a dated MLX edition, update the skill and current links, verify the formula contract in all scenarios, inspect every worksheet and report page, and publish the reviewed changes.

## Acceptance

- The headline opinion is on page one and matches the release record.
- Workbook bridge equations tie, historical periods/bases match, absent or ambiguous source facts fail closed, and investment inventory cannot silently double count other assets.
- Forecast values remain unchanged unless a separate supported assumption revision is made.
- New evidence and interpretations are distinguishable from inherited material. Older editions remain available.
- Numerical checks and visual checks are recorded separately; economic limitations remain explicit.
