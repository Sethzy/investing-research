# Resolve valuation questions with evidence and conditional arithmetic

Scope: deepen the existing research, without another agent framework or changing the accepted operating model silently.

1. Re-read relevant filing notes before declaring a required fact undisclosed. Record new discoveries and corrections.
2. Bridge reported operating cash to cash before working-capital movements, remove investment income/one-offs, apply an explicit tax convention and deduct all reported operating capital spending. Call the result historically adjusted cash, not a cycle-normalized forecast.
3. Test finite operating cash/life combinations with closure and a separate cash/investment bridge. Calculate the annual cash required by the observed price. No perpetual terminal value.
4. For omitted projects, solve the annual cash required to cover assumed capital and a stated value hurdle. Keep economic break-even separate from explaining a market residual. Show delay, ownership and cash-only funding capacity without invented borrowing or dilution.
5. Publish a formula workbook companion, input provenance, independently calculated results and a regenerated dated reader edition. Preserve the accepted operating snapshot and old editions. Conditional tests cannot promote an illustrative model to a price target.
6. Update the investing skill, template reference and tests in the same change. Verify all companion sheets visually and numerically.

Acceptance: actual sold tonnes distinguished from production; historical adjustment reconciles; zero-rate and delayed cash flows tested; no double attribution, double counting of cash or provision-as-cash assumption; publication distinguishes changed analysis from unchanged operating forecast and old X checks.
