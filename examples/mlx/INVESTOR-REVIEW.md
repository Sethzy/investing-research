# MLX investor review pack

**Metals X (ASX:MLX). Research dated 20 September 2026. Opinion: Not rated.**

## Read in this order

1. [Investment review PDF](editions/2026-09-20-valuation-questions/MLX-investment-review.pdf): executive opinion, what changed, cash generation, conditional valuation and funding, then the supporting company, industry and X evidence.
2. [Conditional analysis Excel](editions/2026-09-20-valuation-questions/questions/questions.xlsx): inspect the cash-quality adjustments, annual cash required by the price, and development break-even and funding tests. Inputs and source notes are included.
3. [Original operating Excel](editions/2026-09-20-valuation-questions/model.xlsx): the earlier production-based operating sensitivity, retained unchanged. Its forecast assumptions remain illustrative. It is a separate lens, not another asset value to add to the conditional workbook.

On GitHub, use the download button for each PDF/Excel file, then open it locally. No research installation or X login is required to review this pack. [Read the report online](editions/2026-09-20-valuation-questions/MLX-investment-review.md) if preferred.

## What we would like your judgment on

- **Investment thesis:** What is the strongest reason to own MLX, and the strongest reason to avoid it at the dated reference price?
- **Cash generation:** Are the historical adjustments sensible? Which price, tax, capital or working-capital assumptions would you change before forecasting?
- **Asset and project value:** What evidence would justify a different remaining mine life, investment valuation, or Rentails cash-flow and funding case?
- **Decision:** What specifically prevents a Buy/Hold/Sell view, and which one or two pieces of evidence would most change your assessment?

Please identify the report page or workbook sheet, the assumption you disagree with, and the source or alternative treatment you would use. We are seeking an independent critique, not confirmation of the existing view.

## Scope to keep in mind

The A$2.15 reference quote is dated 18 September 2026; cash, debt and investment carrying amounts are from June. Forward figures in the conditional workbook are explicit tests, not management guidance or a supported price target. Current mine-plan and Rentails engineering/funding details remain unresolved. X excerpts retain their original evidence dates and partial-capture limits; this valuation follow-up did not run a new social search.

The files can be reviewed independently. To run fresh research, use the [repository README](../../README.md#install) and your own Codex workspace and X login. Earlier editions remain in the [dated history](README.md).
