# Reader report and release checks

Write a plain-language investment story with nearby original-source citations and the matching Excel link. Keep code, identifiers and operational logs in supporting files.

Required substantive sections (equivalent clear wording is acceptable):

1. Investment judgment and what changed since the previous review: prior/new view, research cutoff, actual web/X check dates, horizon and unchanged assumptions.
2. Business and historical cash generation: comparable periods, operating mechanism, production-to-cash explanation and reconciliation.
3. Existing-business valuation: included/excluded components, evidence-supported versus assumed inputs, sensitivities and opposing case.
4. Material development projects and funding: for MLX, dedicated Rentails economics, permits, milestones and funding analysis.
5. Industry and contrary evidence: for MLX, dedicated tin market and competing supply case.
6. Investor discussion on X: useful attributed excerpts, original links and short corroboration/valuation analysis.
7. What remains unresolved: readable category coverage, why each material gap matters, next source and what cannot be concluded.

Every important question ends with a supported conclusion, model implication or explicit gap. A gap is an acceptable truthful outcome, not evidence the task was fully analyzed. A small update may link the unchanged foundational analysis rather than repeat it.

Before release, save a coverage-review using docs/evidence-completion.md. Complete source categories even when unavailable or not applicable. Run the report section check, verify cited numbers and links, recalculate the actual workbook and perform the Excel template's visual audit. Retain the previous reader edition before changing the current view. Export-only work retains its source cutoff and search times. Never call partial research comprehensive or use a category count as a quality score.
