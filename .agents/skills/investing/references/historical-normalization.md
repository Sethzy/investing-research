# Historical normalization

Attempt three annual periods and eight quarterly periods, recording the actual periods covered. Eight overlapping documents do not necessarily represent eight distinct quarters. Quarterly operating reports are not audited quarterly financial statements.

Preserve reported figures separately from adjustments and forecasts. For each fact retain original URL, publication date, page, period, entity, units, currency, ownership basis and definition. Preserve conflicting versions and state which is used and why.

For mining, reconcile ore processed × grade × recovery to produced metal, then produced metal to sales/payable metal, inventory, realized receipts and revenue where disclosures permit. Never call production sales or infer contract payability from spot price. Reconcile whole-operation figures to attributable figures exactly once.

Compare like periods. Never divide annual figures by four to invent reported quarters. Identify sustaining versus growth capital; C1 versus AISC inclusions; royalties, depreciation, taxes and selling deductions. Avoid subtracting costs twice.

Reconcile opening cash + operating + investing + financing + FX/other movements to closing cash. Show the unexplained residual and rounding tolerance. Cash flow less selected capital spending is a labelled subtotal, not automatically distributable cash. A tax adjustment alone is not normalized earnings.

For updates use the vendored model-update and earnings-analysis references: prior dated expectation, reported actual, delta, reason, new forecast, valuation consequence. When no defensible prior estimate exists, say so. Do not invent consensus. Capture/read/extract/reconcile/analyze/model-use are separate recorded stages.
