# Source playbook

Always check these categories; never promise universal access or exhaustive coverage. Match authority to the claim. Company and exchange copies of one filing are one source, not independent corroboration.

| Category | First stop | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Filings | Issuer investor relations and listing exchange; SEC EDGAR for US issuers | Actual report, reporting period, publication date, original URL, inspected pages and extracted facts |
| Management | Results presentations, transcripts, guidance and capital-allocation disclosures | Original commitment beside subsequent outcome; distinguish aspiration from guidance |
| Industry | Government statistics, commodity exchange and specialist industry body | Units, observation date, definitions, lag and access limits |
| Peers | Competitors' official filings | Comparable ownership, stage, mine life, geography and cost definitions; explain exclusions |
| Counterevidence | Independent reporting and contrary primary evidence | Strongest opposing explanation and evidence that could disprove the thesis |
| X | Broad identity, asset/industry, relevant preferred handles and contrary searches | Actual query interval, cap/failure, shortlisted exact posts and corroboration |
| Regulatory | Relevant regulator and planning authority | Current decision/document and conditions; application is not approval |

## MLX / Australian tin sources

- [Metals X announcements](https://www.metalsx.com.au/asx-announcements/) and [ASX](https://www.asx.com.au/asx/v2/statistics/announcements.do): reports, mine plans, reserves, shares, commitments and funding.
- [Tasmania EPA Rentails assessment](https://epa.tas.gov.au/business-industry/assessment/proposals-assessed-by-the-epa/bluestone-mines-tasmania-joint-venture-pty-ltd-renison-mine-rentails-project-expansion): assessment stage, environmental documents and conditions. Follow linked decisions and West Coast Council planning records when material. Do not infer approval from an issued guideline.
- [USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/mineral-commodity-summaries): annual supply and reserve baseline; lagged rather than live.
- [International Tin Association](https://www.internationaltin.org/tin-supply/): public industry updates. Label industry affiliation. Paid studies/cost curves are unavailable unless lawfully accessible; never substitute a social quote for the underlying study.
- [LME](https://www.lme.com/Market-data): available dated tin prices and exchange stocks. State cash/three-month contract, currency, units and delay. Exchange inventory is not global inventory. Do not promise free live or full historical data.
- [RBA](https://www.rba.gov.au/statistics/tables/): dated FX and interest-rate series. Preserve FX direction and valuation currency.
- Official filings from comparable tin producers: normalize definitions before comparing costs or multiples.

Refresh issuer/exchange, material regulator developments and X for a research update. Re-export preserves original check dates. Record each failed attempt, try a legitimate official mirror or browser, then name the unresolved question. Do not silently use paywalled previews as full evidence. Stop bounded searches with explicit gaps rather than inventing inputs.
